Built for solo agents

Your deals have enough moving parts. Your workspace shouldn't.

Most solo agents track contract contingencies in email threads, property showings in notes apps, client details inside a brokerage-owned CRM, and actual commission take-home in rough spreadsheets. When deadlines overlap across multiple files, vital details fall through the cracks.

Closing Desk puts every contract milestone, real commission calculation, and exportable client relationship into one calm Notion workspace that stays yours regardless of where you hang your licence.

Stop hunting through PDFs for contingency dates

Every earnest money deposit, inspection window, loan commitment, and lease date sits in one structured tracker sorted by colour-coded urgency.

Know what you keep before closing day

Gross commission numbers can be misleading. Account for brokerage splits, per-deal expenses, and estimated tax reserves right inside each transaction file.

Own your client list if you switch brokers

Take-home Commission Calculator

Gross commission checks look very different after brokerage fees, deal expenses, and taxes. Every transaction calculates your actual net keep so your personal budget reflects reality.

  • Deduct your specific brokerage split automatically
  • Log per-deal costs (photos, staging, admin fees, mileage)
  • Set aside estimated tax percentages per closing

Simple Setup

No complex integrations, API keys, or weeks of configuration. Duplicate the workspace and start organising live transactions right away.

Step 02

Choose the deadline sets relevant to your transactions

Add your deals and start managing your business

Enter your active files, brokerage split, and client details—or delete the sample deals once you're familiar with how everything connects.
